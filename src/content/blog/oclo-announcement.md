Wrote a framework: [Oclo](https://github.com/alestainer/oclo).

Everything is a system. All systems transform. Goals exist only within systems. All choices are temporary. Systems persist through selection.

Three practices follow: capture knowledge that outlasts your context, filter for signal, share with the network.

It's designed to be forked. The only constraint is transparency.
