Built a directory: [SharedPot](https://sharedpot.github.io).

From my work at Solidarity Kitchen Vienna: 4 people can make 150 portions in 3-4 hours. Cooking together can help to get to know your neighbors and form mutual aid networks that are increasingly rare now.

SharedPot is a map of local cooking circles — Telegram, WhatsApp, and Signal groups where people do this. Find one near you, or submit yours.

It also shows 15,915 food pantries and food aid resources across all 50 US states, pulled from public data, for anyone who needs them.
